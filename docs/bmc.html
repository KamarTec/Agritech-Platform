FarmLink — Business Model Canvas

A digital platform connecting farmers, retailers, and investors  |  Ghana  |  2026

Key Partners

  • Paystack (payments & escrow)
  • Google (Gemini AI for crop diagnosis)
  • Farmer cooperatives & market associations
  • Agro-input suppliers
  • Banks & microfinance institutions (farm loans)
  • Logistics/transport providers
  • Cloud providers (DigitalOcean, Cloudflare)
  • Africa's Talking (SMS alerts)

Key Activities

  • Platform development & maintenance
  • Farmer onboarding & KYC verification
  • Escrow & dispute management
  • Marketing & community building
  • Managing harvest investment campaigns
  • Partnership management

Value Propositions

  • Farmers: sell directly with no middlemen, raise harvest funding, free AI crop diagnosis, fair prices
  • Retailers: reliable direct sourcing, post demand requests and receive competitive bids
  • Investors: invest from GHS 100 in real harvests and earn profit shares from verified farms
  • Everyone: escrow-protected payments and Trust Scores — trade without fear of fraud
  • The only platform in Ghana combining direct trade, harvest investment, AI diagnosis & escrow

Customer Relationships

  • Self-service platform with in-app support
  • Trust Scores & verification badges
  • SMS, email & in-app notifications
  • Farmer community & knowledge hub
  • Dedicated account manager (Business plan)
  • Referral rewards programme

Customer Segments

  • Smallholder & commercial farmers in Ghana
  • Retailers, food traders, restaurants & hotels
  • Individual investors (young professionals, students, diaspora)
  • Agro-input suppliers (fertiliser, seeds, equipment)
  • Future: NGOs, research bodies & institutions buying market data

Key Resources

  • The FarmLink platform (web & mobile app)
  • Development & operations team
  • Cloud infrastructure (servers, database, storage)
  • Network of verified farmers, retailers & investors
  • Escrow system & brand trust

Channels

  • Web platform & mobile app
  • Social media (WhatsApp, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram)
  • Radio adverts in farming regions
  • Field visits to cooperatives & markets
  • Posters at agro-shops & market centres

Cost Structure

  • Staff salaries (≈ GHS 8,600/month for a team of 4)
  • Cloud hosting, database & AI API costs (≈ GHS 1,200/month)
  • Marketing, adverts & farmer onboarding campaigns
  • Startup equipment — laptops, phones, office (≈ GHS 39,000 one-time)
  • Business registration, legal & compliance; payment processing charges

Revenue Streams

  • Transaction fees: 2.5% on produce sales, 3% on funded harvest campaigns, 0.5% escrow fee
  • Subscriptions: Farmer Pro GHS 49 / Retailer Pro GHS 79 / Investor Pro GHS 99 / Business GHS 199 per month
  • Boosted listings & promoted campaigns (GHS 20–150)
  • Supplier marketplace fees & group-buy commissions (2%)
  • Insurance pool management fee (10%); loan referral fees; B2B market data reports
